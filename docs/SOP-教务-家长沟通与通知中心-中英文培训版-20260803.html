中文版 · 操作前准备

点击任何按钮前，先完成这四项准备

准备

  • 使用本人账号和岗位对应工作台。
  • 除非主管明确授权，否则只使用培训数据。
  • 准备正确的对象、日期、负责人和所需资料。
  • 把 PDF 与系统并排打开，每次只做一个编号步骤。

禁止

  • 不使用同事账号。
  • 不猜测姓名、金额、日期、状态或缺失字段。
  • 结果不明确时不重复点击保存、应用、批准、发布或发送。
  • 页面不一致时立即停止并询问负责人。

中文版 · 完整步骤总览

家长沟通与通知中心

1进入网页端家长沟通中心，按待处理状态筛选，核对学生、家长、负责人和原始请求。
2阅读全部正文和附件，区分家长原话、内部备注和可以对外发送的内容。
3需要老师反馈时关联正确课次和老师；老师提交后由教务复核学生、事实、语气和隐私。
4确认收件对象、发送渠道和文案后只发布一次；技术发送成功不等于家长已经理解。
5转发、人工补发或家长确认必须在原记录内留痕，不使用私人聊天作为唯一证据。
6内容错误时停止再次发送，使用更正流程记录原内容、更正原因、新内容和最终通知状态。

请按顺序完成

  1. 确认入口：核对页面标题和当前岗位，确保进入的是本文功能，不是相似页面。
  2. 确认对象：核对学生、老师、任务、日期、金额、课包或记录编号。
  3. 执行本步：进入网页端家长沟通中心，按待处理状态筛选，核对学生、家长、负责人和原始请求。

ENGLISH SECTION · BEFORE YOU START

Complete these four checks before clicking

Prepare

  • Use your own account and role workspace.
  • Use training data unless a manager authorises real data.
  • Prepare the correct target, date, owner, and source records.
  • Keep this PDF beside the system and complete one numbered step at a time.

Never

  • Do not use another employee's account.
  • Do not guess names, amounts, dates, statuses, or missing fields.
  • Do not repeat Save, Apply, Approve, Publish, or Send when the result is unclear.
  • Stop and ask the owner when the page differs.

ENGLISH SECTION · WORKFLOW OVERVIEW

Parent Communication and Notification Centre

1Open the Web Parent Communication Centre, filter pending items, and verify the student, parent, owner, and original request.
2Read the full message and attachments, separating the parent's original words, internal notes, and parent-facing content.
3When teacher feedback is required, link the correct session and teacher; after submission, Academic reviews the student, facts, tone, and privacy.
4Verify recipient, channel, and wording, then publish once; technical delivery does not prove parent understanding.
5Record forwarding, manual resend, or parent acknowledgement in the original record; private chat cannot be the only evidence.
6If content is wrong, stop sending, then use the correction flow to record the original content, reason, corrected content, and final notification state.

ENGLISH SECTION · FINAL CHECK

Report completion only when every item is true

Employee self-check

  • The account, platform, role, and target record are correct.
  • Every step was completed in order.
  • The expected final status remains after refresh.
  • Non-sensitive evidence of the result was saved.
  • You can explain who handles an exception.

Stop and escalate

  • Required data or permission is missing.
  • The page, button, or status differs from the guide.
  • An unauthorised action would affect finance, contracts, packages, payroll, live sessions, or parent-visible content.
  • The final state cannot be verified.
