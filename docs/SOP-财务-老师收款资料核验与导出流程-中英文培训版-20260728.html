Verify and Export Teacher Payment Details
老师收款资料核验与导出

1
Open the payroll or payment-details queue and filter the target teacher by period.进入老师工资或收款资料队列，按周期筛选目标老师。
2
For local PayNow verify name, identifier, and status; for Wise verify account and currency.本地 PayNow 核对姓名、号码和状态；海外 Wise 核对账户和币种。
3
Return missing, invalid, or identity-mismatched details to the teacher; do not fill them on the teacher's behalf.缺失、格式错误或身份不一致时退回老师更正，不代填。
4
Before export, verify filters, teacher count, currencies, and sensitive-data handling.导出前确认筛选范围、老师数量、币种和敏感资料处理要求。
5
After payment, record status and proof under Finance rules and retain the audit trail.付款完成后按财务规则记录状态和凭证，保留审计。

Safety and completion checklist / 安全与完成检查

Employee / 员工

  • Use training data only. / 仅使用培训数据。
  • Record start state, key actions, final state, and evidence. / 记录开始状态、关键操作、最终状态和证据。
  • Never enter passwords, tokens, or unrelated personal data. / 不填写密码、Token 或无关个人资料。
  • Submit the practical evidence in Training Centre. / 在培训中心提交实操证据。

Stop and escalate / 停止并升级

  • Required data or permission is missing. / 缺少必要资料或权限。
  • The page differs from the current SOP. / 页面与现行 SOP 不一致。
  • The action affects finance, packages, contracts, payroll, real sessions, or parent-visible content. / 操作影响财务、课包、合同、工资、真实课次或家长可见内容。
  • The final state cannot be verified. / 无法确认最终状态。
Training roles assign learning only and never grant system permissions.
培训岗位只分配学习内容，不授予任何系统权限。
