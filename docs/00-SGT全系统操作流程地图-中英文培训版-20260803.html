中文版 · 操作前准备

点击任何按钮前，先完成这四项准备

准备

  • 使用本人账号和岗位对应工作台。
  • 除非主管明确授权，否则只使用培训数据。
  • 准备正确的对象、日期、负责人和所需资料。
  • 把 PDF 与系统并排打开，每次只做一个编号步骤。

禁止

  • 不使用同事账号。
  • 不猜测姓名、金额、日期、状态或缺失字段。
  • 结果不明确时不重复点击保存、应用、批准、发布或发送。
  • 页面不一致时立即停止并询问负责人。

中文版 · 完整步骤总览

SGT 全系统操作流程地图

1先按业务结果找到主流程和负责人。
2确认自己的岗位、系统入口和对应详细 SOP。
3操作前核对所需资料、权限和停止条件。
4按最终系统状态验收，不以“点过按钮”为完成。
5异常时保留当前状态、证据和下一步并升级主管。

中文流程 · 第 1 步，共 5 步

先按业务结果找到主流程和负责人。

按照截图中的红框或标注找到操作位置。

请按顺序完成

  1. 确认入口：核对页面标题和当前岗位，确保进入的是本文功能，不是相似页面。
  2. 确认对象：核对学生、老师、任务、日期、金额、课包或记录编号。
  3. 执行本步：先按业务结果找到主流程和负责人。
  4. 保存前：逐项复核刚才填写或选择的内容；缺失资料不得猜填。
  5. 保存后：只点击一次保存或提交，然后刷新或重新打开记录。
  6. 完成标志：页面显示预期结果、负责人和最终状态，且结果仍属于正确对象。
立即停止： 姓名、金额、权限、状态或按钮与本教程不一致时，不要继续或重复点击；保留当前页面并联系流程负责人。

中文版 · 最终验收

以下项目全部确认后才能报告完成

员工自查

  • 账号、平台、岗位和目标记录正确。
  • 全部步骤按顺序完成。
  • 刷新后仍看到预期最终状态。
  • 已保存不含敏感信息的结果证据。
  • 能说明出现异常时找谁处理。

停止并升级

  • 缺少必要资料或权限。
  • 页面、按钮或状态与教程不同。
  • 未获授权却会影响财务、合同、课包、工资、真实课次或家长可见内容。
  • 无法确认最终状态。

ENGLISH SECTION · BEFORE YOU START

Complete these four checks before clicking

Prepare

  • Use your own account and role workspace.
  • Use training data unless a manager authorises real data.
  • Prepare the correct target, date, owner, and source records.
  • Keep this PDF beside the system and complete one numbered step at a time.

Never

  • Do not use another employee's account.
  • Do not guess names, amounts, dates, statuses, or missing fields.
  • Do not repeat Save, Apply, Approve, Publish, or Send when the result is unclear.
  • Stop and ask the owner when the page differs.

ENGLISH SECTION · WORKFLOW OVERVIEW

SGT Full-System Operations Map

1Find the main workflow and owner by business outcome.
2Confirm your role, system entry point, and detailed SOP.
3Check required data, permissions, and stop conditions before acting.
4Verify the final system state; clicking a button is not completion.
5For exceptions, preserve the current state, evidence, and next action, then escalate.

ENGLISH WORKFLOW · STEP 1 OF 5

Find the main workflow and owner by business outcome.

Use the red box or callout to locate the action.

Complete in this order

  1. Confirm the entry: Check the page title and current role. Make sure this is the function described here, not a similar page.
  2. Confirm the target: Recheck the student, teacher, task, date, amount, package, or record number.
  3. Perform this step: Find the main workflow and owner by business outcome.
  4. Before saving: Review every field or selection. Never guess missing information.
  5. After saving: Click Save or Submit once, then refresh or reopen the record.
  6. Completion signal: The expected result, owner, and final status remain visible on the correct record.
STOP: If the name, amount, permission, status, or button differs, do not continue or click again. Preserve the screen and contact the workflow owner.

ENGLISH SECTION · FINAL CHECK

Report completion only when every item is true

Employee self-check

  • The account, platform, role, and target record are correct.
  • Every step was completed in order.
  • The expected final status remains after refresh.
  • Non-sensitive evidence of the result was saved.
  • You can explain who handles an exception.

Stop and escalate

  • Required data or permission is missing.
  • The page, button, or status differs from the guide.
  • An unauthorised action would affect finance, contracts, packages, payroll, live sessions, or parent-visible content.
  • The final state cannot be verified.
