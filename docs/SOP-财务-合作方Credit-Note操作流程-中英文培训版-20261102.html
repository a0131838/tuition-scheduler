中文版 · 完整步骤总览

合作方 Credit Note

1从合作方原发票进入 Credit Note，确认公司、发票和可冲减余额。
2填写业务原因、地址、日期和明细，不直接修改原发票金额。
3保存草稿后复核税务口径、明细、总额和原发票关联。
4确认无误后才签发；签发会进入正式财务文件和审计。
5错误草稿可更正；已签发按规则作废，不删除历史。

ENGLISH SECTION · WORKFLOW OVERVIEW

Partner Credit Note

1Start from the original partner invoice and verify company, invoice, and remaining creditable balance.
2Enter business reason, address, date, and line items; do not edit the original invoice amount.
3After saving the draft, verify tax treatment, lines, total, and original-invoice link.
4Issue only after review; issuance creates an official finance document and audit event.
5Correct draft errors; void issued notes under policy and never delete history.
