管理者先记住 / Management principles

01
中文
  1. 系统是双通道：小程序自动 + 微信人工。
  2. 老师原文与家长展示版必须分开。
  3. 管理看积压、时效、质量和异常，不代替一线责任。
  4. “已发送”必须有业务证据。
  5. 错误不删历史，通过更正闭环。
  6. 账号按角色最小授权；双身份用两个账号。
English
  1. The system uses two channels: automatic miniapp and manual WeChat.
  2. Teacher original and parent-facing content remain separate.
  3. Management monitors backlog, timeliness, quality, and exceptions without replacing operational ownership.
  4. Completed sends require evidence.
  5. Do not erase errors; close them through corrections.
  6. Use least privilege; dual-role staff use separate accounts.

反馈质量抽查 / Feedback quality review

05
中文
  1. 每周抽查已发布反馈，覆盖不同老师和学生。
  2. 对比老师原文与家长展示版：事实是否一致。
  3. 检查是否有空泛、复制、敏感或错误对象。
  4. 发现质量问题时，记录培训主题，不直接改历史。
English
  1. Sample published feedback weekly across teachers and students.
  2. Compare original and parent-facing versions for factual consistency.
  3. Check for vague, copied, sensitive, or misdirected content.
  4. Record coaching themes instead of rewriting history.
抽查标准 / Sample standard：事实正确、对象正确、语言清楚、可行动、发布时间及时、人工转发有证据。 / Correct facts and recipient, clear actionable language, timely publication, and evidence of manual delivery.

自动通知监控 / Automatic notification monitoring

06
中文
  1. 进入“小程序通知管理”。
  2. 按状态查看待发送、已发送、失败、跳过/失效。
  3. 失败集中时先查授权、模板和微信接口。
  4. 课程改动后确认旧通知已失效。
  5. 不要把人工已发送当成自动成功。
English
  1. Open Miniapp Notification Management.
  2. Filter pending, sent, failed, and skipped/invalidated rows.
  3. If failures cluster, check authorization, templates, and WeChat API.
  4. After schedule changes, verify stale notifications are invalidated.
  5. Manual success does not mean automatic success.
红框：自动通知汇总与明细。 / Red box: automatic notification summary and rows.

操作日志与责任追溯 / Audit logs and accountability

07
中文
  1. 进入“审计日志 / Audit Logs”。
  2. 按时间、人员、动作、对象筛选。
  3. 核对领取、转交、发布、复制、截图、发送、重试、豁免。
  4. 处理投诉时按时间线复原，不依赖口头记忆。
  5. 日志只用于查错和改进，不用于随意删除责任记录。
English
  1. Open Audit Logs.
  2. Filter by time, actor, action, and target.
  3. Review claim, transfer, publish, copy, evidence, send, retry, and waive.
  4. Reconstruct incidents from timestamps, not memory.
  5. Use logs for recovery and improvement; never erase accountability.
红框：筛选条件和操作时间线。 / Red box: filters and audit timeline.

错误事件处理 / Incident response

08
中文
  1. 先阻止错误继续传播：使旧提醒失效或创建更正。
  2. 确认正确课表/反馈事实。
  3. 由原负责人或值班教务发送更正。
  4. 保留原记录、截图和更正证据。
  5. 查日志确定原因：数据、流程、权限、培训或系统。
  6. 记录后续预防措施。
English
  1. Stop further propagation by invalidating stale reminders or creating a correction.
  2. Verify the correct schedule/feedback facts.
  3. The owner or on-duty staff sends the correction.
  4. Preserve original logs and correction evidence.
  5. Use audit data to classify the cause: data, process, permission, training, or system.
  6. Record preventive action.
禁止 / Prohibited：禁止删除错误任务、改数据库掩盖、让员工换账号重做、只在微信口头说明但不回系统闭环。 / Never delete the error, hide it through database edits, redo under another account, or resolve only in WeChat without system closure.

账号与角色：Jasmine 双身份 / Dual-role account governance

09
中文

Jasmine 同时是管理和老师时，继续使用两个账号密码：管理账号进入 Admin/管理端；老师账号进入 Teacher/老师端。不要把两个权限合并成一个无限权限账号。

排课、审核、转发、权限配置使用管理账号；确认本人课程、点名、反馈、工资使用老师账号。

English

Because Jasmine is both management and teacher, keep two separate credentials: one Admin account and one Teacher account. Do not merge them into one unrestricted identity.

Use Admin for scheduling, review, communication, and access control; use Teacher for personal lessons, attendance, feedback, and payroll.

每日与每周管理检查 / Daily and weekly review

11
中文

每日：需更正、未领取、过期反馈、17:00 未发提醒、自动失败。

每周：反馈质量抽查、按负责人完成量、超时原因、重复更正、授权失败趋势、家长投诉。

每月：账号权限复核、角色变更、日志抽查、SOP 培训更新。

English

Daily: corrections, unclaimed work, overdue feedback, unsent 17:00 reminders, automatic failures.

Weekly: quality samples, workload by owner, delay causes, repeat corrections, authorization failures, complaints.

Monthly: access review, role changes, audit sampling, and SOP refresh.

建议指标 / Suggested metrics：24h 反馈发布率、17:00 前提醒完成率、更正平均处理时间、自动成功率、有截图的人工发送率、无人领取任务数。 / 24h publish rate, reminder completion by 17:00, correction time, auto success rate, evidence coverage, and unclaimed count.
