先记住：6 条硬规则 / Six non-negotiable rules

01
中文
  1. 先确认课程，再上课；时间、学生或地点不对，立即联系教务。
  2. 课后反馈必须针对本节课，不能复制旧内容。
  3. “已提交”不等于“家长已看到”；教务审核发布后才可见。
  4. 老师原文永久保留；教务只编辑家长展示版。
  5. 看到“已退回”必须按原因修改并重新提交。
  6. 工资单按月核对，确认前检查课时、金额和异常。
English
  1. Confirm the lesson before teaching; report any wrong time, student, or location immediately.
  2. Feedback must describe the actual lesson and must not be copied from an old entry.
  3. Submitted does not mean visible to parents; Academic Operations must review and publish it.
  4. Your original text remains unchanged; staff edit only the parent-facing version.
  5. Returned feedback must be corrected and resubmitted.
  6. Verify hours, amount, and exceptions before confirming payroll.

确认课程与异常上报 / Confirm lessons and report issues

03
中文
  1. 打开“我的课程 / My Sessions”。
  2. 逐节核对日期、时间、学生、科目、线上/线下地点。
  3. 确认无误后完成课程确认。
  4. 如有问题，不要自行改课；通过排课协调或联系教务，写清正确安排。
English
  1. Open My Sessions.
  2. Verify date, time, student, subject, and online/offline location.
  3. Confirm the lesson when all details are correct.
  4. Do not change the schedule yourself. Send a scheduling coordination request with the correct details.
培训账号没有真实课程，因此列表为空；正式账号会显示本人课程。 / The training account has no live sessions; your production account will show assigned lessons.
停止点 / Stop condition：学生、时间或地点任一不一致，都不要先点确认。 / Do not confirm if any student, time, or location is incorrect.

课后点名与反馈提交 / Attendance and lesson feedback

04
中文
  1. 课程结束后打开该节课程详情。
  2. 先完成点名；确认出席、请假、缺席状态。
  3. 填写本节内容、学生表现、作业/下一步建议。
  4. 检查是否出现不适合家长的内部措辞。
  5. 提交后状态变为“待审核”。
English
  1. Open the lesson detail after class.
  2. Complete attendance first.
  3. Enter lesson content, performance, homework, and next steps.
  4. Check for unclear or inappropriate wording.
  5. Submit; the status becomes Pending Review.

查看学生过往所有老师反馈 / Review cross-teacher student history

06
中文
  1. 打开“学生反馈 / Student Feedbacks”。
  2. 选择当前带课学生。
  3. 按日期倒序阅读不同老师的历史反馈。
  4. 只用于备课和教学衔接，不得转发给无关人员。
  5. 发现长期重复问题时，在本次反馈中写清变化和下一步。
English
  1. Open Student Feedbacks.
  2. Select a student you currently teach.
  3. Read feedback from all previous teachers in reverse chronological order.
  4. Use it only for lesson preparation and continuity; never forward it externally.
  5. If a pattern repeats, state the change and next step in your new feedback.
红框：学生历史反馈入口与列表区域。 / Red box: cross-teacher feedback history area.

反馈被退回时怎么改 / How to revise returned feedback

07
中文
  1. 打开待办或反馈记录，找到“已退回”。
  2. 先读退回原因，不要只改一个字就提交。
  3. 补足缺失事实：课堂内容、表现依据、作业或下一步。
  4. 不要删除真实问题；改成客观、可执行的表达。
  5. 重新提交并确认状态回到“待审核”。
English
  1. Open the returned item from Tasks or Feedback history.
  2. Read the return reason before editing.
  3. Add missing facts, evidence, homework, or next steps.
  4. Keep genuine concerns but express them objectively and constructively.
  5. Resubmit and confirm the status returns to Pending Review.
示例 / Example：“学生不认真”改为“本节 3 次需要提醒回到题目；建议下节先用 10 分钟复习分数运算”。 / Replace “The student was careless” with observable evidence and a concrete next step.

工资单查看与确认 / View and confirm payroll

08
中文
  1. 进入“财务 / Finance”→“工资 / Payroll”。
  2. 选择月份，核对总课时、每节记录、费率和金额。
  3. 检查取消课、代课、补课和异常调整。
  4. 无误后点击确认；有误先提交问题，不要勉强确认。
  5. 确认后保留系统记录。
English
  1. Open Finance → Payroll.
  2. Select the month and verify total hours, lesson rows, rate, and amount.
  3. Check cancellations, replacements, make-up lessons, and adjustments.
  4. Confirm only when correct; report discrepancies first.
  5. The confirmation remains in the audit trail.
红框：工资核对与确认区域。 / Red box: payroll verification and confirmation area.

小程序操作日志 / Miniapp operation logs

09
中文

小程序中的关键操作会写入日志，包括登录、点名、反馈提交、重新提交、工资确认和排课协调。操作后看到成功提示，再返回列表确认状态。

如果点错了，不要连续重复点击。记下时间、页面、课程/学生和刚才的动作，立即告诉教务。

English

Key miniapp actions are logged: login, attendance, feedback submission/resubmission, payroll confirmation, and scheduling coordination. Wait for the success message and verify the resulting status.

If you make a mistake, do not keep tapping. Record the time, page, lesson/student, and action, then contact Academic Operations.

不要做 / Do not：不要用另一个账号“修正”，不要删除聊天证据，不要重复提交相同反馈。 / Do not switch accounts to “fix” a mistake, delete evidence, or submit duplicates.

常见错误与正确处理 / Common mistakes

10
错误 / Mistake正确处理 / Correct action
提交后看不到家长端这是待审核状态；联系教务查看，不要重复提交。 / It is pending review; ask Academic Operations, do not duplicate.
课程时间不对提交排课协调/联系教务，不自行修改。 / Submit scheduling coordination; do not self-edit.
学生历史为空确认是否选择正确学生、是否具有带课关系；仍为空联系管理员。 / Verify student and teaching relationship, then contact Admin.

小程序按钮无反应等待 5 秒，返回重进一次；记录时间和页面并上报。 / Wait five seconds, reopen once, then report with time and page.
