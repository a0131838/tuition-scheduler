Partner Credit Note
合作方 Credit Note

1
Start from the original partner invoice and verify company, invoice, and remaining creditable balance.从合作方原发票进入 Credit Note，确认公司、发票和可冲减余额。
2
Enter business reason, address, date, and line items; do not edit the original invoice amount.填写业务原因、地址、日期和明细，不直接修改原发票金额。
3
After saving the draft, verify tax treatment, lines, total, and original-invoice link.保存草稿后复核税务口径、明细、总额和原发票关联。
4
Issue only after review; issuance creates an official finance document and audit event.确认无误后才签发；签发会进入正式财务文件和审计。
5
Correct draft errors; void issued notes under policy and never delete history.错误草稿可更正；已签发按规则作废，不删除历史。

Stop and escalate / 停止并升级

  • Required data or permission is missing. / 缺少必要资料或权限。
