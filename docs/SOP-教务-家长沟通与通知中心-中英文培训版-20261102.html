准备

  • 使用本人账号和岗位对应工作台。
  • 除非主管明确授权，否则只使用培训数据。
  • 准备正确的对象、日期、负责人和所需资料。

禁止

  • 不使用同事账号。

中文版 · 完整步骤总览

家长沟通与通知中心

1打开家长沟通与通知中心，选择课后反馈和待审核状态，核对学生、课程及负责人。
2展开审核反馈，区分老师原文和家长展示版，检查完整内容及隐私。
3核对反馈对应的课次和老师；内容不完整时退回老师补充。
4确认对象和内容正确后审核并发布到家长端；收件人身份不明时先核实。
5按机构流程完成微信群人工转发，并在原任务中记录实际发送结果。
6发现内容错误时停止再次发送，保留记录并联系负责人按规定更正。

ENGLISH SECTION · BEFORE YOU START

Complete these four checks before clicking

Prepare

  • Use your own account and role workspace.
  • Use training data unless a manager authorises real data.
  • Prepare the correct target, date, owner, and source records.
  • Keep this PDF beside the system and complete one numbered step at a time.

Never

  • Do not use another employee's account.
  • Do not guess names, amounts, dates, statuses, or missing fields.
  • Do not repeat Save, Apply, Approve, Publish, or Send when the result is unclear.
  • Stop and ask the owner when the page differs.

ENGLISH SECTION · WORKFLOW OVERVIEW

Parent Communication and Notification Centre

1Open Parent Communication, choose lesson feedback and the pending-review filter, then verify the student, course and owner.
2Expand Review feedback, distinguish the teacher original from the parent-facing version, and check completeness and privacy.
3Check the lesson and teacher; return incomplete feedback to the teacher.
4After verifying the recipient and content, approve and publish to the parent app; resolve uncertain recipient identity first.
5Complete manual WeChat forwarding under the organisation’s process and record the actual result in the task.
6If content is wrong, stop forwarding, preserve the record and contact the responsible manager for the approved correction process.

ENGLISH SECTION · FINAL CHECK

Report completion only when every item is true

Employee self-check

  • The account, platform, role, and target record are correct.
  • Every step was completed in order.
  • The expected final status remains after refresh.
  • Non-sensitive evidence of the result was saved.
  • You can explain who handles an exception.

Stop and escalate

  • Required data or permission is missing.
  • The page, button, or status differs from the guide.
  • An unauthorised action would affect finance, contracts, packages, payroll, live sessions, or parent-visible content.
  • The final state cannot be verified.
