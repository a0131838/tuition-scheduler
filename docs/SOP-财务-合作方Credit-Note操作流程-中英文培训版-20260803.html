准备

  • 使用本人账号和岗位对应工作台。
  • 除非主管明确授权，否则只使用培训数据。
  • 准备正确的对象、日期、负责人和所需资料。
  • 把 PDF 与系统并排打开，每次只做一个编号步骤。

禁止

  • 不使用同事账号。
  • 不猜测姓名、金额、日期、状态或缺失字段。
  • 结果不明确时不重复点击保存、应用、批准、发布或发送。
  • 页面不一致时立即停止并询问负责人。

中文版 · 完整步骤总览

合作方 Credit Note

1从合作方原发票进入 Credit Note，确认公司、发票和可冲减余额。
2填写业务原因、地址、日期和明细，不直接修改原发票金额。
3保存草稿后复核税务口径、明细、总额和原发票关联。
4确认无误后才签发；签发会进入正式财务文件和审计。
5错误草稿可更正；已签发按规则作废，不删除历史。

ENGLISH SECTION · BEFORE YOU START

Complete these four checks before clicking

Prepare

  • Use your own account and role workspace.
  • Use training data unless a manager authorises real data.
  • Prepare the correct target, date, owner, and source records.
  • Keep this PDF beside the system and complete one numbered step at a time.

Never

  • Do not use another employee's account.
  • Do not guess names, amounts, dates, statuses, or missing fields.
  • Do not repeat Save, Apply, Approve, Publish, or Send when the result is unclear.
  • Stop and ask the owner when the page differs.

ENGLISH SECTION · WORKFLOW OVERVIEW

Partner Credit Note

1Start from the original partner invoice and verify company, invoice, and remaining creditable balance.
2Enter business reason, address, date, and line items; do not edit the original invoice amount.
3After saving the draft, verify tax treatment, lines, total, and original-invoice link.
4Issue only after review; issuance creates an official finance document and audit event.
5Correct draft errors; void issued notes under policy and never delete history.
