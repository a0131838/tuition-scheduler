How to Fill in Payment Details / 如何填写收款资料

本流程只给老师使用。新的收款资料只支持 PayNow 和 Wise：本地老师填写 PayNow，海外老师填写 Wise。银行转账资料只保留历史查看，不再作为新填写方式。
This guide is for tutors only. New payment details support PayNow and Wise only: local tutors use PayNow, overseas tutors use Wise. Bank-transfer details are kept for legacy viewing only and are no longer a new submission method.

对象 / Audience老师 / Tutors
路径 / PathTeacher Portal -> Finance -> Payment Details
版本 / Version29 May 2026

一句话 / Short Answer

打开 Payment Details / 收款资料，选择 PayNow 或 Wise (overseas tutors)，填写对应资料，最后点 Save Payment Details / 保存收款资料。
Open Payment Details, choose PayNow or Wise (overseas tutors), fill in the matching fields, then click Save Payment Details.

先记住 / Must-Know Rules

  1. 本地老师请选择 PayNow。 / Local tutors should choose PayNow.
  2. 海外老师请选择 Wise (overseas tutors)。 / Overseas tutors should choose Wise (overseas tutors).
  3. 不要再新增 Bank Transfer；历史银行资料只用于查看。 / Do not submit new Bank Transfer details; legacy bank details are view-only.
  4. 保存后会显示 Finance Review = Pending Review，表示等待财务核验。 / After saving, Finance Review = Pending Review means Finance has not verified it yet.
  5. 如果财务打回 Rejected，请按原因重新填写并保存。 / If Finance marks it Rejected, update the details based on the reason and save again.

流程总览 / Workflow

  1. 登录 Teacher Portal。 / Log in to the Teacher Portal.
  2. 从左侧 Finance / 财务进入 Payment Details / 收款资料。 / Open Payment Details from Finance in the sidebar.
  3. 先看顶部当前资料和 Finance Review。 / Check the current details and Finance Review status at the top.
  4. 选择 PayNow 或 Wise。 / Choose PayNow or Wise.
  5. 填写对应字段。 / Fill in the matching fields.
  6. 点击 Save Payment Details。 / Click Save Payment Details.

Step 1: 打开收款资料页 / Open Payment Details

Payment Details

进入页面后，先看顶部卡片。这里会显示老师编号、当前收款方式、财务核验状态、当前 PayNow/Wise 和历史银行账号。
After opening the page, check the top card first. It shows your tutor code, current payment method, finance review status, current PayNow/Wise details, and legacy bank account.

图 1 / Figure 1: 这是系统页面内容截图，不包含浏览器标签栏。先看 Finance Review，再选择收款方式。 / This is a system-content screenshot without browser tabs. Check Finance Review first, then choose the payment method.

如果选择 PayNow / If You Choose PayNow

字段 / Field怎么填 / What to enter
Payment Method选择 PayNow / PayNow。 / Choose PayNow.
PayNow Type选择 Mobile、NRIC/FIN、UEN 或 Other。 / Choose Mobile, NRIC/FIN, UEN, or Other.
PayNow ID / Mobile填写 PayNow 注册手机号或 ID，不要填写银行账号。 / Enter the PayNow-registered mobile number or ID. Do not enter a bank account number.
PayNow Name填写收款时显示的名字。 / Enter the name shown to the payer.
Note如果收款名和老师姓名不同，请写说明。 / If the payment name is different from your tutor name, explain it here.
重要 / Important: PayNow ID 不是银行账号。填错会导致财务无法核验或延迟付款。 / PayNow ID is not a bank account number. Wrong details may delay Finance verification and payout.

Step 2: 海外老师填写 Wise / Overseas Tutors Fill Wise

Wise

海外老师选择 Wise 后，应提供可以让财务在 Wise 查找到你的信息：Wise 邮箱、Wise 手机号、WiseTag / username 至少一种。
After choosing Wise, overseas tutors should provide information Finance can use to find them in Wise: at least one of Wise email, Wise phone, or WiseTag / username.

图 2 / Figure 2: 海外老师选择 Wise，并填写 Wise 资料。保存后等待财务核验。 / Overseas tutors choose Wise and fill in Wise details. After saving, wait for Finance review.

如果选择 Wise / If You Choose Wise

字段 / Field怎么填 / What to enter
Wise Account Holder NameWise 户名。 / Wise account holder name.
Wise EmailWise 绑定邮箱或收款邮箱。 / Wise login or receiving email.
Wise PhoneWise 绑定手机号，最好包含国家区号。 / Wise-linked phone number, preferably with country code.
WiseTag / Wise Username如果有 WiseTag，请填写。 / Enter your WiseTag or Wise username if you have one.
Country / Currency所在国家和希望收款币种。 / Your country and preferred receiving currency.
Wise Note其他说明，例如只能用邮箱查找。 / Extra notes, for example “can only be found by email”.
建议 / Tip: Wise Email、Wise Phone、WiseTag 至少填一种。资料越完整，财务付款越快。 / Fill at least one of Wise Email, Wise Phone, or WiseTag. More complete details help Finance pay faster.

常见错误 / Common Mistakes

  • 本地老师选择 Wise，但没有 Wise 账号。 / Local tutor chooses Wise without having a Wise account.
  • 海外老师只填写普通银行账号，没有填写 Wise 邮箱、电话或 WiseTag。 / Overseas tutor only gives a normal bank account and does not fill Wise email, phone, or WiseTag.
  • PayNow ID 填成银行账号。 / PayNow ID is filled with a bank account number.
  • 收款名和老师姓名不一致，但备注没有说明。 / Payment name differs from tutor name, but no explanation is written.
  • 看到历史银行资料，以为还可以新增银行转账。 / Tutor sees legacy bank details and assumes new bank transfer is still allowed.

特殊情况 / Special Cases

Finance Review 是 Pending / Pending Review

表示财务还没有核验。保存后这是正常状态。 / Finance has not verified it yet. This is normal after saving.

Finance Review 是 Rejected / Rejected

看财务备注，修改后重新保存。 / Read the Finance note, update the details, and save again.

换手机号或 Wise 账号 / Changed Phone or Wise Account

请在发工资或报销前更新。 / Update it before payroll or reimbursement processing.

看不到 Payment Details / Cannot See Payment Details

联系 Admin 检查老师账号是否绑定老师档案。 / Ask Admin to check whether your account is linked to a teacher profile.

保存前检查 / Final Checklist

  1. Payment Method 已选择 PayNow 或 Wise。 / Payment Method is selected as PayNow or Wise.
  2. 本地老师：PayNow Type、PayNow ID、PayNow Name 已填写。 / Local tutor: PayNow Type, PayNow ID, and PayNow Name are filled.
  3. 海外老师：Wise 邮箱、手机、WiseTag 至少一种可查找信息已填写。 / Overseas tutor: at least one searchable Wise detail is filled: email, phone, or WiseTag.
  4. 如果名字不一致，已在 Note / Wise Note 写说明。 / If the name differs, the reason is written in Note or Wise Note.
  5. 点击 Save Payment Details。 / Click Save Payment Details.
  6. 保存后看到 Finance Review 等待财务核验。 / After saving, Finance Review shows it is waiting for review.
安全提醒 / Security reminder: 收款资料是敏感信息，不要发在普通群聊里。 / Payment details are sensitive. Do not send them in normal group chats.
