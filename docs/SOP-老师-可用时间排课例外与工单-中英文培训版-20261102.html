中文版 · 完整步骤总览

老师可用时间、排课例外与工单

1进入“我的可上课时间”，先检查未来62天覆盖天数和已有时段；这些是真实排课依据，不是个人备忘。
2使用单日或批量日期新增准确时段；保存前核对日期、开始和结束时间，避免重叠或错误时区。
3需要移除当天可用时段时使用清空当天；误清空可撤回最近一次操作，遇到已有课程时联系教务。
4只在“排课例外确认”回复分配给自己的请求，选择可以、不可以或建议替代时间，并写清可执行的具体时间。
5老师的回复不是正式改课；返回课表确认教务是否已建立或更新正式课次，未变化时不要重复提交。
6在老师工单中先筛选本人任务，核对学生、情况、优先级和凭证；完成后填写可核对的完成说明再标记完成。

ENGLISH SECTION · WORKFLOW OVERVIEW

Teacher Availability, Scheduling Exceptions, and Tickets

1Open My Availability and check the next-62-day coverage and saved ranges; these drive real scheduling and are not personal notes.
2Add accurate ranges by single day or date range; verify dates, start and end times, overlaps, and timezone before saving.
3Use Clear day to remove that day’s saved slots. Undo the latest clear if needed; contact Academic when existing sessions prevent clearing.
4Reply only to assigned Scheduling Exceptions, choose Can do, Cannot do, or Suggest another slot, and provide a specific actionable time.
5A teacher reply does not change the official session; return to My Sessions and verify Academic created or updated it, and do not resubmit when unchanged.
6In Teacher Tickets, filter assigned work, verify student, situation, priority, and evidence, then add a verifiable completion note before marking complete.
