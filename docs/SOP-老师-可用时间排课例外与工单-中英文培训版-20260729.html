END-TO-END WORKFLOW / 完整操作流程

Teacher Availability, Scheduling Exceptions, and Tickets
老师可用时间、排课例外与工单

1
Open My Availability and check the next-30-day coverage and saved ranges; these drive real scheduling and are not personal notes.进入“我的可上课时间”，先检查未来30天覆盖天数和已有时段；这些是真实排课依据，不是个人备忘。
2
Add accurate ranges by single day or date range; verify dates, start and end times, overlaps, and timezone before saving.使用单日或批量日期新增准确时段；保存前核对日期、开始和结束时间，避免重叠或错误时区。
3
Use Clear Day when an entire day becomes unavailable; undo only the latest eligible clear-day action and stop for Academic help when uncertain.需要整天不可用时使用清空当天；误清空只撤销最近一次可撤销操作，无法确认时停止并联系教务。
4
Reply only to assigned Scheduling Exceptions, choose Can do, Cannot do, or Suggest another slot, and provide a specific actionable time.只在“排课例外确认”回复分配给自己的请求，选择可以、不可以或建议替代时间，并写清可执行的具体时间。
5
A teacher reply does not change the official session; return to My Sessions and verify Academic created or updated it, and do not resubmit when unchanged.老师的回复不是正式改课；返回课表确认教务是否已建立或更新正式课次，未变化时不要重复提交。
6
In Teacher Tickets, filter assigned work, verify student, situation, priority, and evidence, then add a verifiable completion note before marking complete.在老师工单中先筛选本人任务，核对学生、情况、优先级和凭证；完成后填写可核对的完成说明再标记完成。
